# Tea Industry Peer Comparison

A self-led equity research project comparing three listed Indian tea companies
(Tata Consumer Products, Goodricke Group, United Nilgiri Tea Estates) over FY21 to FY26.

## Question
Is the branded tea model more resilient than the plantation (grower) model?

## Method
- Financial data from Screener.in (Data Sheet exports), in ₹ crore
- Calculated in Excel: sales growth, EBITDA margin, net margin, ROE, asset turnover, P/E
- Industry context from Tea Board of India data

## Files
- Tea_Industry_Peer_Comparison_Report.pdf: 4-page report with charts and conclusions
- Tea_Peer_Comparison_Analysis.xlsx: ratio workbook with formulas

## Key finding
Tata Consumer showed the steadiest growth and margins. Goodricke's margins were thin
and it posted a loss in FY24. United Nilgiri was highly profitable but small and
partly depends on investment income.

25th October 2026. Educational project, not investment advice.
